## Across all steps

**Accepted method**

- Report what was done at every stage, with a flow diagram of records identified, screened, excluded (with reasons) and included. *PRISMA 2020: Page MJ et al. BMJ 2021;372:n71* (https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.n71)
- The authors stay accountable for AI use; justify and validate a tool for the specific review, keep human oversight, and report any AI that makes or suggests judgements. *AI in evidence synthesis (RAISE): Joint position statement of Cochrane, Campbell, JBI and CEE, 2025* (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12594113/)

**In Sieve**

- ✓ Every AI step is a fixed prompt + JSON schema, stored per run with model and date, next to the human decision.
- ✓ Evidence quotes are checked word for word against the paper and can be opened highlighted in the text.
- ◐ The methods draft (step 8) names the models used; a complete AI-use statement (tool, version, validation results, human oversight) is not generated yet.

## 1 · Protocol

**Accepted method**

- Define the question and eligibility criteria before searching, and register or publish the protocol (e.g. PROSPERO, OSF). *PRISMA-P: Moher D et al. Syst Rev 2015;4:1* (https://doi.org/10.1186/2046-4053-4-1)
- Scoping reviews: the same a priori approach (population, concept, context) with their own reporting checklist. *PRISMA-ScR: Tricco AC et al. Ann Intern Med 2018;169:467-73* (https://doi.org/10.7326/M18-0850)

**In Sieve**

- ✓ Question, PICO / PICo / SPIDER / PCC, eligibility criteria with the consequence of failing each one.
- ✓ Start from an existing document (ticket, proposal, criteria flowchart).
- ✗ Export in PRISMA-P / registration format and a frozen, dated protocol version.

## 2 · Search

**Accepted method**

- Peer review of the MEDLINE strategy before translation, on six elements: translation of the question, Boolean/proximity operators, subject headings, text words, spelling/syntax/line numbers, limits and filters. *PRESS 2015: McGowan J et al. J Clin Epidemiol 2016;75:40-6* (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclinepi.2016.01.021)
- Test the strategy against a set of known relevant studies (relative recall). *Relative recall: Sampson M et al. BMC Med Res Methodol 2006;6:33* (https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2288-6-33)
- Search at least Embase, MEDLINE, Web of Science and Google Scholar; add subject databases (e.g. CINAHL, PsycInfo) when the topic fits. *Database combinations: Bramer WM et al. Syst Rev 2017;6:245* (https://doi.org/10.1186/s13643-017-0644-y)
- Report every database with its platform, the full strategy, limits, search date and number of records. *PRISMA-S: Rethlefsen ML et al. Syst Rev 2021;10:39* (https://doi.org/10.1186/s13643-020-01542-z)

**In Sieve**

- ✓ Validation and hold-out sets, tested live in PubMed (recall, missed articles and why), and recall per database after importing the exports.
- ✓ Translations of a MEDLINE (Ovid) strategy to Embase, Web of Science, PsycInfo and ACM, with a check list and an assistant review; counts per line.
- ◐ PRESS: syntax checks and an assistant review, but no structured PRESS 2015 checklist for a second searcher.
- ◐ PRISMA-S: strategies and counts per line are kept; platform, search date and limits per database are not recorded as fields yet.
- ✗ Google Scholar (first 200 results), CINAHL and Scopus translations.

## 3 · Records and deduplication

**Accepted method**

- Remove duplicates in fixed passes from strict to loose field combinations, with normalised page numbers; check the loose matches by hand. *Bramer deduplication: Bramer WM et al. J Med Libr Assoc 2016;104(3):240-3; correction 2017;105(1):111* (https://doi.org/10.3163/1536-5050.104.3.014)
- Report records identified per source and the number of duplicates removed. *PRISMA 2020: Page MJ et al. BMJ 2021;372:n71* (https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.n71)

**In Sieve**

- ✓ Bramer passes A-G plus DOI/PMID, run after every import; strict matches linked automatically, the rest in a review list (docs/deduplication.md).
- ✓ Duplicates are linked, not deleted; overlap per database (already found / only here).

## 4 · Verify records

**Accepted method**

- Check whether included reports have been retracted or corrected, and identify the version of a study that is reported (preprint vs. journal article). *Cochrane Handbook: Higgins JPT et al. (eds). Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions, current version* (https://training.cochrane.org/handbook)

**In Sieve**

- ✓ DOI ↔ title ↔ year checks against Crossref, shared wrong links, erratum and supplement flags.
- ✓ Preprints recognised, with the published version found and used.
- ✗ Automatic retraction check (Crossref / Retraction Watch data).

## 5 · Full text

**Accepted method**

- Retrieve the full text of every record that passes title/abstract screening; report reports sought and reports not retrieved. *PRISMA 2020: Page MJ et al. BMJ 2021;372:n71* (https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.n71)

**In Sieve**

- ✓ Automatic retrieval from open sources (Europe PMC, PubMed Central, arXiv, Unpaywall), a download list for the rest, PDFs or a whole folder matched by content.
- ✗ "Reports not retrieved" as a separate number in the PRISMA flow diagram.

## 6 · Screening

**Accepted method**

- Single-reviewer title/abstract screening misses studies (median 5%, 13% for less experienced reviewers): screen in duplicate, independently, with conflict resolution. *Single vs. dual screening: Waffenschmidt S et al. BMC Med Res Methodol 2019;19:132* (https://doi.org/10.1186/s12874-019-0782-0)
- An AI screener needs validation against human decisions on this review (sensitivity first) and human oversight; report it. *AI in evidence synthesis (RAISE): Joint position statement of Cochrane, Campbell, JBI and CEE, 2025* (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12594113/)
- With prioritised (active learning) screening, use an explicit, conservative stopping rule. *SAFE stopping procedure: Boetje J, van de Schoot R. Syst Rev 2024;13:81* (https://doi.org/10.1186/s13643-024-02502-7)

**In Sieve**

- ✓ Model runs per criterion with verified quotes; sensitivity, specificity and κ against your decisions; disagreements first; full-text exclusions require a reason.
- ◐ A model can be the second reviewer, but a true two-human mode (blinded, independent, with conflict resolution) is not there yet.
- ✗ Prioritised screening with a stopping rule such as SAFE.

## 7 · Data extraction

**Accepted method**

- Single extraction produces more errors than double extraction; extract key (outcome) data independently in duplicate or at least verify every value. *Single vs. double extraction: Buscemi N et al. J Clin Epidemiol 2006;59:697-703* (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16765272/)
- Pilot the extraction form on a few studies before extracting everything. *Cochrane Handbook: Higgins JPT et al. (eds). Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions, current version* (https://training.cochrane.org/handbook)
- Assess quality / risk of bias with a tool that fits the study type: e.g. PROBAST+AI for (AI) prediction models, RoB 2, ROBINS-I, QUADAS-2. *PROBAST+AI: Moons KGM et al. BMJ 2025;388:e082505* (https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2024-082505)

**In Sieve**

- ✓ Every value with a verified quote; two runs compared field by field; the human verifies each cell (disagreements and unverified quotes first).
- ✓ Pilot run and run checks (completeness, verified quotes, core-only rule).
- ✗ Risk-of-bias / quality appraisal module (e.g. PROBAST+AI, QUADAS-2) as a form like extraction.

## 8 · Report

**Accepted method**

- Flow diagram and the 27-item checklist. *PRISMA 2020: Page MJ et al. BMJ 2021;372:n71* (https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.n71)
- Search appendix with every full strategy. *PRISMA-S: Rethlefsen ML et al. Syst Rev 2021;10:39* (https://doi.org/10.1186/s13643-020-01542-z)
- A transparent statement of AI use and its validation. *AI in evidence synthesis (RAISE): Joint position statement of Cochrane, Campbell, JBI and CEE, 2025* (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12594113/)

**In Sieve**

- ✓ PRISMA 2020 counts and diagram, a methods draft built from what was run, a search appendix with all strategies and translations.
- ✗ PRISMA 2020 checklist export, PRISMA-S checklist export, a complete AI-use statement.
